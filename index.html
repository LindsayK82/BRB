Our house rules 💚

We've sent the full welcome email to your inbox. Before you chat, please review these rules.

1. No bullying, harassment, or doxxing. Threatening other members with doxxing, rape, stalking, or murder may result in an immediate permanent ban.

2. Swear freely. Just don't weaponize it. This is an adult community. Swear about your day, not at each other.

3. Keep it respectful. Debate ideas and politics, but don't insult or intimidate other members.

4. Don't be an asshole. That's our Golden Rule.

Everyone starts with five private stars. A human administrator—not a bot or AI—reviews reported comments. Removing a rule-breaking comment costs one star. You can appeal within seven days from your account. At zero stars, you cannot chat but can submit eligible appeals.
